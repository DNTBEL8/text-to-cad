## Top categories — ranked by profit/effort ratio

### 1. Desk & office organizers ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
**Why:** Evergreen demand. Remote work drove permanent growth. Repeat buyers.
- Cable clips, wire managers, charger docks
- Pen/pencil holders, desk caddies
- Monitor stand with storage, headphone hook
- Keyboard tray, laptop riser

Material cost: $0.40–$1.50 | Sell price: $8–$25 | Margin: ~75%

**Generate prompt examples:**
- "minimalist desk cable clip that mounts under desk edge"
- "stackable desk organizer with phone slot and pen holder"
- "under-desk headphone hook with cable routing slot"

---

### 2. Home & kitchen organizers ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
**Why:** Huge Etsy audience. Pinterest-driven impulse buys. Gift-friendly.
- Spice rack (wall-mount or drawer insert)
- Modular drawer dividers
- Bag clip / food sealer clip
- Fridge magnet organizer (hooks, notepad holder)
- Cord wrap / cable organizer

Material cost: $0.50–$2.00 | Sell price: $10–$30 | Margin: ~70%

---

### 3. Plant pots & vases ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
**Why:** Indoor plant trend is huge in 2026. Buyers want unique shapes.
- Geometric / lattice planters
- Self-watering pot insert
- Hanging wall planter
- Succulent tray (multiple pods)
- Propagation station (test tube vase)

Material cost: $0.50–$3.00 | Sell price: $12–$40 | Margin: ~72%

**Note:** Must be printed solid or with inner shell sealed for water. Vases use vase mode (single wall spiral) — very fast prints.

---

### 4. Tabletop gaming miniatures ⭐⭐⭐⭐
**Why:** $12B market, growing to $25B by 2028. Passionate buyers, high willingness to pay.
- Terrain pieces (walls, ruins, dungeon tiles)
- Generic monster/creature minis
- Scatter terrain (barrels, crates, trees)
- Tokens, markers, condition rings

Material cost: $0.30–$1.50 | Sell price: $8–$25 | Margin: ~75–85%

**Critical:** Resin printer preferred for fine detail. FDM OK for terrain/scatter.
**Legal:** Never sell copyrighted IP (D&D monsters, Warhammer, etc.). Original designs only.

Material cost: $0.40–$1.50 | Sell price: $10–$20 | Margin: ~70%

## What NOT to make (avoid these pitfalls)

| Item | Why to avoid |
|---|---|
| Fidget spinners | Saturated, commoditized |
| Copyrighted characters | Copyright infringement risk |
| Weapons / weapon parts | Platform bans, legal risk |
| Anything needing support structures only | High failure rate, bad reviews |
| Ultra-complex assemblies | Too much post-processing for a farm |
| Items < $8 sell price | Fees eat your margin |

## Where to sell — platform comparison

| Platform | Fee | Best for | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Etsy** | ~10–12% total | Home decor, gifts, custom | Largest handmade audience. Requires original designs. Start here. |
| **Amazon Handmade** | 15% | Functional/household items | High traffic, harder to stand out |
| **eBay** | 12–15% | Niche/technical items | Good for replacement parts, gaming terrain |
| **Facebook Marketplace** | Free (local) | Fragile items, local pickup | No shipping = no breakage |
| **Shopify (own store)** | 2–3% + $30/mo | Brand building, repeat customers | Best margins, but you drive traffic |
| **Cults3D** | 20% | STL files (not physical prints) | Sell the design file, not the print |
| **MyMiniFactory** | 25% | Gaming miniatures, STL files | Huge tabletop audience |

**Recommended path:**
1. **Start:** Etsy (built-in traffic, test what sells)
2. **Scale:** Add Amazon Handmade for winning products
3. **Brand:** Build Shopify store once you have repeat customers

## Pricing formula

```
Price = (material_cost + electricity + wear) × 4  +  labor  +  platform_fee_buffer
```

Simplified for FDM farm:
```
Price = material_cost × 10   (minimum floor for low-cost items)
Price = material_cost × 6–8  (for items with higher material cost)
```

**Never price below:** material_cost × 4 (covers costs + fees, no profit)
**Target:** 30–40% net margin after all fees and costs.

## Etsy SEO quick tips

- Include material in title: "3D Printed Bamboo-Look Planter"
- Include use case: "Desk Cable Organizer for Standing Desk"
- Add dimensions in listing (buyers search for these)
- Use all 13 tag slots
- 5+ photos minimum (different angles + in-context lifestyle shot)
- Offer customization (color, size) = higher conversion
